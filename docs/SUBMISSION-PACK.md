## Project name
Emmaus Care: Pastoral Visit Companion

## Track

## Short description (gallery)
A calm AI companion that helps pastors and care teams prepare for a pastoral visit in minutes: a visit brief, gentle conversation prompts, Scripture and prayer options, and a follow-up plan a person always approves. Private by design, everything runs on the device.

## Long description
In many small churches, one pastor carries every hospital visit, every grief call, and every homebound member, often driving straight from work with no time to prepare. Emmaus Care gives that pastor ten quiet minutes of preparation, so the person being visited receives presence instead of hurry.

Choose a kind of visit: hospital, grief, homebound, new baby, anxiety or crisis, or someone finding their way back. Add a first name and a few honest lines. Emmaus Care drafts the heart of the visit, three conversation openers, a listen-for list tuned by the details in your notes, three Scripture options from public domain translations, a named prayer you can edit and pray aloud, the common mistakes to avoid, practical notes, and a dated follow-up plan that reaches the quiet weeks when support usually fades, including the birthday and the anniversary of a loss.

Responsible AI is the design, not a footnote. Notes never leave the device: all processing is local, and the app asks for a first name only. Every plan is labelled a draft for a human to review. The tool never diagnoses, never promises healing outcomes, and points to emergency and professional help where safety is at stake. Its drafting engine is a curated pastoral library built during the hackathon window, honest about what it is, with no external model calls and no data collection.

Named for the road to Emmaus (Luke 24), where a companion listened first and spoke second.
