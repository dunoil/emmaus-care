Why Emmaus Care exists. In many small churches, one pastor carries every hospital visit, every grief call, and every homebound member, often driving straight from work with no time to prepare. Emmaus Care gives that pastor ten quiet minutes of preparation, so the person being visited gets presence instead of hurry.

Named for the road to Emmaus, where a stranger became a companion who listened first and spoke second (Luke 24). Scripture quotations are short excerpts from the public domain King James Version and World English Bible. Built for the AI for Church Hackathon 2026.
