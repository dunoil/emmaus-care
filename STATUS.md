## Verified rules
- Free, 100 percent online, worldwide, 18+, solo or team of any size.
- Build window: 30 Oct to 27 Nov 2026. Only work in that window is judged. New standalone projects preferred.
- Project must be built with AI or clearly feature AI.
- Submission needs: project name, description, track (exactly one), demo video 5 minutes max. Live demo link and code repository optional but encouraged.
- Judging: Ministry Impact 30 percent, Execution and Quality 25 percent, Innovation 20 percent, Faithfulness and Responsible AI 15 percent, Presentation 10 percent.
- Prizes: Grand Prize $1,500, Runner-Up $500, People's Choice $200, Hacker's Choice $100, Best UI Design $100, Best Ministry Impact $100, plus Best in Track recognition and a D2RM in-kind package for one project.
- A project can win an overall or track award and also Best UI Design or Best Ministry Impact.
- Every participant receives 100 free Lovable credits.
- Responsible AI rules: use synthetic or authorized demo data only, never real congregant personal data. Keep humans in the loop.
